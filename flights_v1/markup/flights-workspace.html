Flight plans are filed with Leidos Flight Service under your own 1‑800‑WX‑BRIEF account, so you can also manage them by phone, on 1800wxbrief.com, or in other apps. Your name, phone, and home base go into every flight plan you file.

1800wxbrief account

Enter your 1800wxbrief.com username (usually your email address). No password needed — you authorize Falken from your Leidos account instead. Don’t have an account? Create one free at 1800wxbrief.com first.

One more step: authorize Falken

Your account isn’t authorized for filing through Falken yet. On another device (or after leaving the app):

  1. Log in at 1800wxbrief.com
  2. Open the Account tab
  3. Choose Service Provider Authorization
  4. Authorize Falken Avionics, then come back here
